## Overview
The goal of this proposal is to outline a strategy for adding cloud deployment capabilities to enhance the software modernization effort. This will provide improved scalability, flexibility, and operational efficiency while addressing the current technological limitations.

## Business Motivation
- Enhance operational efficiency and reduce infrastructure costs.
- Improve scalability to accommodate variable workloads.
- Facilitate quick deployment and updates, leading to faster time-to-market.
- Support remote workforce and provide better accessibility.

## Scope
### In Scope
- Analysis of existing application architecture for cloud readiness.
- Selection of a cloud service provider (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP).
- Implementation of cloud deployment workflows.
- Training for the team on cloud tools and practices.
- Migration of relevant services to the cloud.

### Out of Scope
- Major feature development or redesign of existing software outside cloud requirements.
- Complete rebuild of the application; focus is on adding cloud capabilities only.
- Non-cloud-related infrastructure preparations.

## Stakeholders
- Product Management Team
- Development Team
- IT Operations Team
- Cloud Solution Architect
- End-users (for feedback on performance post-deployment)

## Success Criteria
- Successful migration of identified services to the cloud.
- Achieve a minimum of 20% reduction in operational costs post-deployment.
- Scalability tests show improved performance metrics under load.
- Team successfully completes training on new cloud tools and processes.

## Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk**: Integration issues with existing systems during migration.
  - **Mitigation**: Conduct thorough pre-migration testing and create backup plans.
  
- **Risk**: Team resistance to new technologies and workflows.
  - **Mitigation**: Offer training sessions and continuous support throughout the transition.

- **Risk**: Unforeseen costs associated with cloud deployment.
  - **Mitigation**: Engage in detailed budgeting and resource planning before the transition.

## Timeline Estimate
- Initial assessment and planning: 2 weeks
- Selection of cloud provider and architecture design: 3 weeks
- Implementation and migration of services: 4-6 weeks
- Training and adjustment phase: 2 weeks
- Total estimated timeline: 11-13 weeks

The audit logging story will adhere to high-quality principles focusing on transparency, accuracy, and performance. All logs must be immutable, ensuring non-repudiation, and follow company policies regarding retention and access control. Logs must be comprehensive yet exclude sensitive personal data, complying with security and privacy standards such as GDPR. Logging must be performed asynchronously to reduce impact on system performance. Stakeholder expectations include clear documentation of log structures and the provision of easily accessible dashboards for log monitoring and auditing.

All regulatory and compliance documentation must be clear, unambiguous, and validated for completeness and consistency. Scope statements related to PCI DSS or security compliance must:
- Use language aligned with industry standards, referencing authoritative frameworks (e.g., PCI DSS).
- Clearly state boundaries and exclusions (e.g., no storage, processing, or transmission of Cardholder Data).
- Be reviewed for technical accuracy regarding architecture, data flows, and interactions.
- Receive sign-off from both technical and compliance stakeholders.
- Be versioned and stored in a discoverable, auditable manner in the repository root or an appropriate compliance or documentation directory.
- Ensure sensitive information is not inadvertently committed or disclosed.
- Take into account future maintainability so periodic reviews and updates are practical.

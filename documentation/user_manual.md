# User Manual: Wishlist Feature

## Overview
This section of the manual provides guidance on the newly integrated wishlist feature in the CarePay application, which allows users to save products for future reference or purchase.

## Accessing the Wishlist
- Users must be logged in to access and modify their wishlist.

## Adding a Product to Your Wishlist
1. Navigate to the product page of the desired item.
2. Ensure you are logged into your account.
3. Look for the "Add to Wishlist" button on the product page.
4. Click the "Add to Wishlist" button. A confirmation message will appear indicating the product has been added.

## Removing a Product from Your Wishlist
1. Access your wishlist via the user menu or dedicated wishlist page.
2. Locate the product you wish to remove.
3. Click the "Remove" button next to the product.
4. A confirmation message will appear indicating the product has been removed.

## Viewing Your Wishlist
- You can view your wishlist by navigating to the "Wishlist" section in your account dashboard. Here, you can see all the products you have saved for later reference.

## FAQ
- **Q: Do I need to be logged in to use the wishlist feature?**
  - A: Yes, the wishlist feature is available only to logged-in users.
  
- **Q: Can others see my wishlist?**
  - A: No, your wishlist is private and only visible to you when logged in.

This user manual aims to assist you in effectively utilizing the wishlist feature and enhancing your shopping experience on CarePay.

# PCI DSS Scope Statement

## Executive Summary

This document serves as the PCI DSS Scope Statement for the MVP release of the ApiGateway system. It confirms that the ApiGateway does not store, process, or transmit Cardholder Data (CHD) or Sensitive Authentication Data (SAD). This declaration aims to exempt the system from most PCI DSS requirements, reducing compliance burdens and aligning with our risk assessment procedures.

## Scope Declaration

- **System Exclusion**: The ApiGateway system does not engage in any storage, processing, or transmission activities involving CHD or SAD.
- **Architecture and Data Flows**: The current architecture and data flows confirm the absence of CHD or SAD.
- **Future Considerations**: Any planned features or changes that might affect this scope in future versions are currently out-of-scope.

## Compliance Assurance

This document is intended for compliance auditors, risk managers, and security stakeholders. It will be routinely referenced and updated in alignment with compliance reviews and architectural changes.

## Future Scope Reviews

- Future reviews will assess any new features that may alter this scope.
